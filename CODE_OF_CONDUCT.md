# Community code of conduct

[Русская версия](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ru.md)

Everyone participating in this project's issues, pull requests, Wiki and discussions
is expected to treat others with respect. Discuss technical choices with evidence;
make room for different experience levels and constructive disagreement.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, impersonation, spam and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not publish
credentials, private source code or security exploit details in public reports.

The maintainer, [@MrAliev](https://github.com/MrAliev), may edit or remove harmful content,
limit participation or block an account to protect the community. Responses should be
proportionate to impact, repetition and context. A participant may explain an apparent
misunderstanding and request reconsideration through the same private reporting channel.

For harassment or spam, use GitHub's **Report content** control on the relevant content.
Report safety or privacy concerns privately using the repository's **Security → Report
a vulnerability** channel, stating that it is a community-conduct report. Do not use a
public issue to disclose private details. The maintainer handles reports confidentially
to the extent needed to investigate and protect participants; absolute confidentiality
cannot be promised. Vulnerability reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

These are the project's own community rules; they are not a claim of certification by
or affiliation with another code-of-conduct organization.
